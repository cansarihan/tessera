# Tessera — Demo video script (~90 seconds)

A tight, end-to-end walkthrough for the Level 5 demo video. Record at 1280×720+, screen + voice.

## 0:00 — Hook (landing)
> “This is Tessera — event ticketing on Stellar where tickets **can’t be faked** and scalpers
> **can’t gouge**.”
- Show the landing page. Hover the holographic ticket. Click **Launch app**.

## 0:12 — Create an event (organizer)
- Go to **Organize**. Connect wallet (Freighter).
- Name: “Soroban Summit 2026”. Add two tiers: General (1 XLM ×100), VIP (3 XLM ×20).
- Resale cap: 110%. Click **Create event** → approve in wallet.
> “One transaction creates the event and its tiers on-chain.”

## 0:32 — Buy a ticket (fan)
- Open the event from **Browse**. Pick **VIP**, buy → approve.
> “The ticket mints straight to my wallet as a unique NFT.”
- Go to **My tickets** — show the holographic ticket with its **QR code** and serial number.

## 0:50 — Check in at the door (organizer)
- Open **Check-in scanner**. Scan the ticket QR (or enter the ticket id).
> “The app verifies ownership on-chain and marks the ticket used — it can’t be reused.”
- Show the green **Valid — admitted** result.

## 1:05 — Proof of attendance + analytics
- Go to **My badges** — show the POAP badge minted on check-in.
- Go to **Analytics** — show live sales, check-in rate, revenue and user feedback.

## 1:20 — Resale & close
- Briefly show **Resale market** — “every listing is capped, so no scalping”.
- Close on stellar.expert showing the contract’s transactions.
> “Everything you saw is public and verifiable on Stellar. That’s Tessera.”

## Tips
- Pre-fund 2 testnet wallets (organizer + fan) before recording.
- Keep the API running so Analytics shows live numbers.
- Trim dead air around wallet popups in post.
